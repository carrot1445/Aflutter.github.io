Readings

How do I get a good reading?

  • Rest your fingerprint flat over the camera so it covers the lens completely.
  • A soft touch reads best.
  • Keep still and breathe normally. It takes a few seconds to lock on.
  • Warm hands help. Cold fingers make the signal faint.

Why does my number look off?

Aflutter is for fun, and camera readings are never as exact as a medical device. Movement, cold hands and bright light can all throw a reading off. If something seems wrong with your health, please see a doctor rather than relying on the app.

Playing together

How do we start a live session?

One of you starts a session and shares the six character code or QR code. The other enters or scans it. Once both hearts appear, you're in.

My partner's heart froze or disappeared.

Their connection dropped or their finger slipped off the camera. It comes back on its own once they reconnect and settle their fingerprint.

Subscriptions

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Aflutter. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I paid but features are still locked.

Tap Restore in the app while signed in to the same Apple ID you bought with. If that doesn't work, email us.

Your data

How do I delete my data?

Email AflutterSupport@gmail.com with your display name and we'll delete your account and everything connected to it within 45 days.

Does Aflutter record my camera?

No. Camera frames are processed on your phone in real time and immediately thrown away. Nothing is saved or uploaded. Read more in our Privacy Policy.
