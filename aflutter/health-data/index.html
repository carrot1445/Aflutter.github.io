Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026

This policy applies to consumer health data collected by the Aflutter app, made by Praneel Siddireddy. It is provided under Washington's My Health My Data Act and similar state laws.

1. Consumer health data we collect, and why

  • Heart rate in beats per minute
  • Heartbeat timing, meaning when each beat was detected
  • Reading quality, meaning signal quality and calibration progress
  • Challenge results, meaning how your heart rate changed during a challenge

We collect and use this data only to provide the features you ask for: showing your own readings, showing both partners' readings during a live session you start or join, and showing challenge results and history. We do not use it for advertising, marketing or any other purpose.

2. Where it comes from

  • Directly from you, through your phone's camera when you rest your fingerprint over it. Camera images are processed on your device and are never stored or uploaded.
  • From the other person in a live session you join, whose readings are sent to your phone so you can see them.

3. Consumer health data we share

All four categories listed in section 1, but only as described in section 4.

4. Who we share it with

  • Other Aflutter users in your live session, when you choose to start or join one.
  • Service providers that process data on our behalf: cloud database hosting (Google Firebase).

We do not have affiliates. We do not sell consumer health data.

5. Your rights and how to use them

You have the right to:

  • confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data, and access it, including a list of everyone we've shared it with and how to contact them
  • withdraw your consent to our collection and sharing of it
  • have it deleted, including from backups

To make a request, email AflutterSupport@gmail.com with your display name and what you'd like us to do. You don't need to create an account. We may ask for information needed to confirm the request is yours. We will respond within 45 days, free of charge. If we need more time, we'll tell you why within those 45 days, and we may extend once by up to 45 more days.

When we delete your data, we also notify our service providers so they delete it too.

6. Appeals

If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision, or emailing AflutterSupport@gmail.com, with the word "Appeal" in the subject. We will tell you in writing within 45 days what we did and why. If we deny your appeal, you can contact the Washington State Attorney General at atg.wa.gov/file-complaint.
