Aflutter

Privacy Policy

Your heart, your information.

Effective October 5, 2026

The short version. Aflutter reads your heart rate through your phone's camera. The camera images are processed on your phone and are never saved or sent anywhere. We store only what the app needs to work, we never sell your information, and we never use it for advertising.

This policy explains what the Aflutter app ("Aflutter", "we", "us") collects, how we collect it, how we use it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. Aflutter is made by Praneel Siddireddy, an independent developer based in California, United States. Contact us any time at AflutterSupport@gmail.com.

We also publish a separate Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy describing how we handle heart rate data.

Aflutter is for entertainment. It is not a medical device and its readings should never be used to make decisions about your health.

1. What stays on your phone

  • Camera images. When you rest your fingerprint over the camera, Aflutter measures tiny changes in colour in each frame to estimate your heart rate. Every frame is processed in real time on your device and discarded immediately. We never record, store or upload camera images or video.
  • QR codes. If you scan an invite code, the image is decoded on your device and not kept.
  • Motion sensor. Aflutter reads the motion sensor during a reading to tell how steady the phone is. This stays on your device.
  • Your setup answers and first reading are also saved on your device so the app can pick up where you left off.

2. What we collect and why

Health data

Aflutter collects the following health data, which it derives from your phone's camera:

  • heart rate in beats per minute
  • the timing of each detected heartbeat
  • signal quality and calibration progress
  • challenge results, such as how much your heart rate changed during a challenge

We use it only to show you and the people in your live session your readings and results. We never use it, or anything derived from the camera, for advertising, marketing or data mining, and we never share it with anyone for those purposes.

Your account

When you open Aflutter we create an anonymous account with a random identifier. No email or phone number is needed. If you choose Sign in with Apple, we receive the name and email address Apple shares with us, which may be a private relay address if you choose "Hide My Email". We use it to keep your account and history across devices.

What you tell us

  • The display name you choose, and your partner's name if you enter it. Please only enter someone else's name if they're happy for you to.
  • Your answers to the optional questions during setup: your relationship stage, how long you've been together, whether you live together, what you're curious about, and how you heard about Aflutter. We use these to personalise the app and the subscription screen.

Live sessions

To show both hearts on both phones, a live session sends the following through our database while it runs: the room code, the names of the people in it, the health data above, which challenge is running with its settings and timing, and connection status such as when someone joined or disconnected.

Anyone who joins with your room code can see this information for that session. Only share your code with the person you want to play with.

Linking with a partner

If you link with a partner, we store each person's account identifier and name, when you linked, the invite code used, and which questions you've already seen, so the app doesn't repeat them.

App integrity

Firebase App Check, which uses Apple's App Attest, confirms that requests come from the real Aflutter app so we can block fake or abusive traffic.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment card or billing details. We receive your subscription status so we can unlock the features you paid for.

We never sell your information. We never share it for advertising, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites. We share information only with:

4. How long we keep it

We keep your account, partner links and session history for as long as you use Aflutter, so your history keeps working. When you ask us to delete your information, we delete it from our systems and tell our service providers to delete it too, without undue delay and within 45 days. Information stored only on your phone is removed when you delete the app.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data. Email AflutterSupport@gmail.com with your display name and we will delete your account and everything connected to it.
  • See, export or correct your data. Email us and we'll send you a copy or fix anything that's wrong.
  • Withdraw consent. You can stop Aflutter collecting heart rate data at any time by not taking readings, turning off camera access in iPhone Settings, or asking us to delete your data. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect anything we did before you withdrew it.
  • Setup questions are optional. You can skip them.

We respond to requests within 45 days. If we decline a request, you can appeal by replying to our decision with the word "Appeal". We'll answer your appeal in writing within 45 days.

If you live in California, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose, to delete it, to correct it, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in California law, and we don't use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you.

If you live in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, Praneel Siddireddy is the controller of your information. We process your account and session information because it's needed to provide the app you asked for. Heart rate is health data, and we process it only with your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time as described above. You can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or export your information, or object to its processing, and you can complain to your local data protection authority. Your information is processed in the United States by the providers listed above.

6. Do Not Track

Aflutter doesn't track you across other apps or websites, and no third party collects information about your activity over time and across other services through Aflutter. Because of that, there's nothing for a Do Not Track signal to switch off, and Aflutter doesn't respond to those signals differently.

7. Children

Aflutter is not meant for anyone under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used Aflutter, contact us and we'll delete their information.

8. Security

Information travels over encrypted connections, and our database rules let each person write only their own information. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your room codes private.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect, and we'll ask for your consent again before using health data in any new way.
